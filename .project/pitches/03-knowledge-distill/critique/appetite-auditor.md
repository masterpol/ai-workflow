# Appetite-auditor critique of selective source loading

Pre-bet review of [re-shape-retrieval.md](../re-shape-retrieval.md) with [plan.md](../plan.md).

**Verdict:** keep big-batch, at its small end. I project about 6 product files and 550–800 LOC including tests and docs. The small-batch caps are 5 files and 400 LOC; the big-batch caps are 15 files and 1,500 LOC (`.claude/agents/appetite-auditor.md`). The projection exceeds both small-batch caps and uses about half of the big-batch LOC cap, so no decomposition is needed. The file count covers the three R1 paths plus `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` and `VERSION`. It excludes pitch, catalog and eval records. The LOC range is a judgment from comparable modules, not a measurement of this design.

# Appetite-auditor critique: workspace-slim + knowledge-distill

Both pitches are declared **big-batch** (cap: 15 files / 1500 LOC). Projections below count every new or touched file and LOC, conservatively rounding up from similar shipped scripts in `ai-framework/scripts/`.

| ID | Pitch | Severity | Type | Finding | Suggestion |
|----|-------|----------|------|---------|------------|
| A1 | slim | high | scope-overrun | Projected ~14 files touched and ~2200 LOC for a 15-file/1500-LOC cap. The breadboard lists 4 vendor adapters, `slim.mts` + test, `graphify.mts` + test changes, a new archive script + test, a process-graph entry, doctor list, decision entry and the existing dataset (`workspace-slim/pitch.md` lines 105-110). Comparable scripts (`pitch-compress.mts` 725 LOC, `pitch-archive.mts` 429 LOC, `graphify.mts` 838 LOC, `graphify.test.mts` 678 LOC) show the implementation alone exceeds the cap before adapters, decision entry and doctor edits are counted. | Split out the read-only `report` + `text-to-load` scopes first (each small-batch), then ship the recovery/apply scope as a second pitch. |
| A2 | distill | high | scope-overrun | Projected ~12 files touched and ~1700 LOC for a 15-file/1500-LOC cap. The breadboard adds `distill.mts` + test, 4 adapters, a process-graph entry, doctor list, decision entry and alias support in the graph (`knowledge-distill/pitch.md` lines 33-51). The statement ledger, independent-check gate and alias plumbing are each comparable to a standalone script (`pitch-compress.mts` 725 LOC; `outcome-record.mts` 312 LOC). | Ship the candidate-discovery/ledger design as a spike first, then a small-batch "one batch end-to-end" build; defer full alias plumbing until the sibling slim recovery ships. |
| A3 | both | medium | borderline-tandem | The domain split is right (slim shrinks files/records; distill condenses knowledge meaning), but both pitches individually sit at or above the cap, so running them tandem does not create headroom (`workspace-slim/pitch.md` lines 98-103; `knowledge-distill/pitch.md` lines 80-83). | Keep the split, but sequence them after a shared foundation pitch that extracts the recovery record + restore-and-compare proof so neither pitch carries that cost. |
| A4 | slim | high | hidden-scope | The "first, separately" ignore fix for `.project/skills/packages/` is still inside this pitch's plan (`workspace-slim/pitch.md` lines 107-109). It touches graph ignore lists, `.gitignore` templates, `SETUP.md` and the doctor, adding files and LOC on top of the already-at-cap count. | Pull the ignore fix into its own small-batch pitch or hotfix; it is independent and unblocks measurement before any slim work starts. |
| A5 | slim | medium | reuse-missed | The recovery record and restore proof proposed in slim overlap heavily with `pitch-archive.mts` (byte-copy archive + manifest + verify + restore) and `ledger-schema.mts` (group entries bound by tree hash). A new `slim-archive.mts` would duplicate that machinery (`pitch-compress/SKILL.md` lines 120-156; `pitch-archive.mts` lines 62-100; `ledger-schema.mts` lines 1-38). | Extend `pitch-archive.mts` with a Git-commit recovery format instead of creating a second archive script; reuse `ledger-schema.mts` for group entries. |
| A6 | distill | medium | dependency-risk | distill's apply step relies on workspace-slim's recovery rule and restore proof, which it defines neither (`knowledge-distill/pitch.md` lines 80-83; `workspace-slim/pitch.md` lines 98-103). Until slim ships, distill cannot safely remove originals, so the tandem has a hard ordering dependency. | Add an explicit "slim recovery shipped" milestone gate to distill's plan; do not bet distill until slim's recovery scope is at least in `/plan` with a stable contract. |
| A7 | both | medium | cross-pitch-overlap | Both pitches touch the same shared surfaces: `graphify.mts`/`graphify.test.mts` (index compacting and aliases), `ai-framework/workflow/relations.toon` (new skill entries), `ai-framework/scripts/workflow-doctor.mts` (skill list), and vendor skill adapters (`relations.toon` lines 4-97; `workspace-slim/pitch.md` lines 105-110; `knowledge-distill/pitch.md` lines 33-35). `greenfield-kickoff/pitch.md` also adds adapters and touches the doctor/relations surface. | Consolidate the adapter/doctor/relations edits into one coordination scope or assign one pitch to own each shared file to avoid merge conflicts. |
| A8 | both | medium | eval-load | Each pitch declares 6 golden eval cases, all `specified-not-run`, with two live-only cases each (`workspace-slim.json` lines 18-104; `knowledge-distill.json` lines 18-102). The live-only cases and the independent-checker design imply unbounded human-in-the-loop cost that the 15-file/1500-LOC cap does not capture. | Convert at least the report and ledger-validation cases into automated tests before betting; cap live-only cases to one per pitch. |

## Bottom line

Neither pitch fits a single big-batch. The cleanest path is:

1. Small-batch/hotfix: ignore `.project/skills/packages/` in graph, `.gitignore` and `SETUP.md`.
2. Big-batch **workspace-slim-core**: report + text-to-load (knowledge index, backlog resolved items), staying under cap by deferring recovery/apply.
3. Big-batch **workspace-slim-recovery** (or fold into extended pitch-compress): Git-commit recovery record + restore proof.
4. Big-batch **knowledge-distill** after (3), reusing the recovery proof and a trimmed statement-ledger scope.

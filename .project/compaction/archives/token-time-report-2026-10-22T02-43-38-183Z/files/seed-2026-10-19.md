## Problem

With tokens attributed (`token-attribution`) and time and flow captured (A1, A2), the owner still has no single view per pitch, then phase, then scope of tokens, active versus wait time, effort and efficiency.

## Seed scope

- A new read-only joiner, `ai-framework/scripts/flow-report.mts` plus a test, reading both stores under one lease acquisition and writing `.project/metrics/flow-report.{json,md,html}`; `/state` links it with one line in `state-pages.mts`.
- Join on exact equality of pitch, phase and scope (nested maps, no string join); `(other)`, `(unknown)` and `(unattributed)` never join; legacy events count as `unjoinable`.
- Content: tokens by vendor and effort mix; active time and wait (wait a lower bound with coverage counts); transitions, repeats, failures and the trace; ratios with a per-vendor cache-hit formula so vendors with different `input` meanings are never pooled; output over input; tokens per scope with the unit named; tokens per shipped pitch with ship evidence from `state-deep-evidence`'s history or `unavailable` on truncation.

## Rulings carried from critique

- Token totals and by-model rows live in `state.json` (`state-deep-evidence`); the AI `tokens` slot reads `state.json` only; this pitch owns per-flow rows only.
- `state-deep-evidence` owns record-derived audit, must-fix and revision counts; this pitch keeps event-derived `transitions` only.
- `token-attribution`'s resolver is the single owner of the key.
- Effort per step needs `token-attribution` flow leaves to store per-effort rows; check this before `token-attribution` S7, otherwise this pitch needs a further token snapshot revision.
